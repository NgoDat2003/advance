Summary: 7 Important and 10 Minor verified findings. The most serious: re-running `09-ledger#ddl` silently loses the balanced-ledger trigger; the guide repeats a false "every ALTER TABLE takes AccessExclusiveLock" rule; the float-money evidence (0,25 USD) is a rounding artifact; the 3 s deadlock race likely flips the victim in DBX; there are answer-key and table-name collisions in stages 3/4/7; and hw-check H8 misgrades both ways.
