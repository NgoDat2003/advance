## 2. Contested decisions

**(a) Slide 23 chip.** Change to `MySQL đúng, PG khác`, and back it with a measurement. Reason: the chip rule the guide itself enforces (V04: worst claim on the slide wins) and the precedent of slides 22/24/25 (advice holds, MySQL syntax differs → DIFF). Fallback if the controller wants no new recording: keep `Đúng trên PG16` but rewrite the deviation rationale honestly ("42601 is measurable; chip reflects the slide's advice, not its syntax") so the deviation is a recorded exception rather than a false claim.

**(b) Ledger race sleep.** Raise `pg_sleep(3)` to `pg_sleep(6)` in both regions of `09-ledger-race.A.sql` (and the header's "trong 3 giây"), matching every other two-session lab. Mechanism: each waiter runs the deadlock check exactly once, `deadlock_timeout` (1 s) after it starts waiting. A is the victim only if B is already waiting at least 1 s before A wakes, i.e. B1 − A1 < sleep − 1. With 3 s the human window is < 2 s (switch tab, select, run); with 6 s it is < 5 s. `lock_timeout = '10s'` in those regions still covers the 6 s + 1 s wait. Then: re-record `unordered` and `ordered` (plus try1–3) with `run-lab.sh`; update the `b.m` values in `stage-09.html:332` and quiz 9.4; reword the "Vì sao nạn nhân là A?" box as a rule, not an outcome: "nạn nhân là phiên có đồng hồ deadlock_timeout nổ sau khi vòng khép; chạy B trong 5 s đầu thì đó là A; chạy B ở giây 5–6 thì là B; sau 6 s không có deadlock, chạy lại". Keep the quiz answer as "đúng một 40P01" and drop "nạn nhân A" as a fixed fact.

**(c) lock_timeout for the two waiting tabs.** `SET lock_timeout = '15s'` in `04-index-online.B.sql#cic` and in both tabs of the stage-3 challenge answer. The waits are bounded by A's 6 s sleep plus human latency; 15 s is 2.5× that, still finite (a forgotten tab A does not hang DBX forever), and one rule for both labs. Do not use `'0'`: an unbounded wait is a worse surprise in DBX. Add a comment in B#cic: "CIC phải chờ A commit; nếu lock_timeout ngắn hơn thời gian A ngủ, CIC bị hủy 55P03 và để lại index INVALID (DROP INDEX CONCURRENTLY rồi tạo lại)". Fix quiz 4.4 `why` ("Người ghi không làm CIC lỗi" → "người ghi không bị CIC chặn, nhưng CIC chờ họ; đặt lock_timeout ngắn thì chính CIC bị hủy"), and soften stage-04:89 so the "SET lock_timeout = '5s' ở đầu mọi migration" rule carries the CIC caveat. Re-record `04-index-online.cic.*` (the SQL text is echoed in the run).

**(d) `btree_gist` in `lab07_modeling`.** Not a defect. Nothing outside the schema depends on it, the spec confines writes to `lab07_modeling`, and `99-cleanup` promises to remove the whole lab. Optional: one header line in `99-cleanup.sql` ("cũng gỡ extension btree_gist; nếu schema khác đã dùng EXCLUDE gist, kiểm tra pg_depend trước"). No code change required.

**(e) Append-only ledger.** Add the guard, not just prose: a 4-line `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE ON ledger_entries` trigger that `RAISE EXCEPTION 'sổ cái chỉ ghi thêm: sửa sai bằng bút toán đảo'`. It is cheap because the `ddl` region and its run are being re-recorded anyway (U1), it makes "DB tự chặn" true, and it ties directly to the `refund()` challenge (a reversing entry is the correct correction). Add one sentence in stage-09 prose and EXAM 8. If the controller prefers zero DDL change, the minimum is to soften "DB tự chặn" to "DB chặn bút toán lệch lúc INSERT; sổ cái phải append-only (REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE) để bất biến đứng vững".
